## Verdict

- **Precision: perfect.** 0 false positives posted across all five PRs. The one
  confirmed finding (committed test-report files in nexus#1128) is real and still
  on master.
- **Recall: gated by our own verifier, not by the lenses.** The lenses generated
  24 candidates including genuinely real issues on 4/5 PRs — then the Haiku
  verifier killed 23 of them, including the single best find of the whole set:
  the nexus#1128 circular import (`dbpass → services/iam/__init__ → account_service
  → dbpass`, the "tests/auth-alone gotcha"), rejected twice with **factually wrong
  Python import semantics**. Judges logged 5 wrong rejections, all Haiku
  rationalizations.
- **Head-to-head vs CodeRabbit** (oracle#17): loss, 0–3. All three misses are one
  blind spot: no CI/workflow-security lens (job-scoped `id-token`,
  `secrets: inherit`, nginx location shadowing). CodeRabbit's top-severity claim
  (arm64 image "incompatible") was wrong for this ARM64 cluster, consistent with
  its noise reputation.
- **Vs humans** (nexus#1127): rough parity on substance — Greybeard surfaced 2 of
  the 3 substantive human findings as candidates (verifier killed both as
  judgment calls) and found 1 real docstring inaccuracy the humans missed.
- **Coverage story:** 2 of 5 PRs merged with effectively no review at all
  (CodeRabbit rate-limited). Greybeard produced real findings on both.

## Fixes, ranked (Phase 2)

1. **Verifier tier upgrade** — Haiku demonstrably invents wrong rejection
   rationales. Move verification to the lens model at low effort (or Sonnet 5).
   Verify calls are short; the cost delta is small and it un-suppresses the
   pipeline's best work.
2. **Pack-size cap + degraded-mode honesty** — packs over the verify model's
   context silently kill all verification and still render "You shall pass."
   Cap/truncate the pack per tier, and when verification is unavailable, say so
   in the comment (findings posted as unverified or an explicit degraded notice)
   — never silent.
3. **Add a CI/infra lens** — workflows, Dockerfiles, nginx/proxy config,
   permissions scoping. Erases the entire oracle#17 loss.
4. **Verifier prompt fix** — several rejections *conceded the claim was true*
   ("the docstring is stale… but out of scope") and rejected anyway; forbid
   rejecting findings whose factual claim the verifier confirmed, and stop
   citing PR-body "known gap" notes as grounds for rejection.
